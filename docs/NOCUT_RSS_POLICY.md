# Regional direct RSS — owner-private reader

Added `nocut-jeonnam` and `nocut-gwangju` to the existing 07:00 / 16:00 KST collection. No new scheduler, paid service, key, preference, push, article-page fetch, or AI API call is introduced.

- Feed URLs: `https://rss.nocutnews.co.kr/news/jeonnam.xml` and `https://rss.nocutnews.co.kr/news/gwangju.xml`.
- Permission reference: `https://www.nocutnews.co.kr/rss/` (personal/noncommercial RSS reader use). AI restriction reference: publisher footer at `https://www.nocutnews.co.kr/` (AI learning/use prohibited). Publisher article hosts disallow automated agent article access; this integration never requests them.
- Store/display only RSS title, date, source and canonical article link, plus existing rule-based topic/region fields. Discard descriptions, content, images and author data. No AI analysis or summary of these articles, including their headlines.
- Exact article-link allowlist: HTTPS `jn.nocutnews.co.kr/news/<digits>` for Jeonnam and `gj.nocutnews.co.kr/news/<digits>` for Gwangju. Other hosts/paths fail closed. Query and fragment are discarded; article pages are never fetched.
- Prefer strictly valid ISO `dc:date`; otherwise parse the numeric-month RSS date with explicit day/month/year/time, weekday and KST checks. Invalid, overflow, future and stale dates follow existing rejection rules. The feed's live `dc:date` format was not inspected in this implementation.
- Feed classification establishes only province-level publisher scope (`전남` / `광주`), not a verified event city. Headline city hints and arbitrary category city tags cannot satisfy a city quota. Candidate visibility separately allows a verified publisher province overlapping a selected child city, so city users can see broad provincial news (including More). Such cards disclose “광역 소식 · 시·군 미확인”; they never satisfy city quotas or city-confirmed outage report counts. Unrelated provinces are excluded. Existing minimum-two-local / one-KEPCO selection rules and no-stale-fill policy remain unchanged.
- `/api/automation-summary` preserves reserved five IDs, ordering, slot and request ID. All sources outside MOTIR/EIA/PJM are represented by `{id, sourceId, metadataRedacted:true}`; raw title, URL and date are omitted. `reviewable`, `candidates` and `verified` reapply the same strict source allowlist, including old stored reservations. Stored selection and human-facing `/api/briefing` retain their original data. The automated reader must use the summary route, never the human briefing. When candidateCount is zero and events.reviewDue is false, it exits quietly after the existing usage ledger step without further news/event reads or writes.
- `/api/editorial-workflow` contains static workflow configuration; `/api/editorial` validates its existing official-source allowlist. No sixth AI article is added to replace a restricted selection.

Validation uses synthetic XML only. No actual RSS titles, article URLs, descriptions or images were read by the implementation agent or included in tests. Live feed ingestion and regional article availability are not asserted by synthetic tests; a valid province feed may still fail to supply city-specific quota articles.
